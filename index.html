Paper journal checklist

Write and date at least 1 entry every week. Keep a dedicated paper notebook; digital notes must be copied into it.

Keep at the front

A table of contents, a reading list including seminar and beyond, a list of people consulted, and seminar assignments by week. Date and index entries.

Minimum reflection counts

  • 3 on articles/books and their implications for your project or LDT.
  • 3 on design-challenge meetings, including at least 2 expert conversations.
  • 3 after learner interviews and/or observations.
  • 1 after meeting each teaching-team member, for 3 total; office hours or co-working can count.
  • Additional open-ended reflections are welcome.

What the readable rubric looks for

Completeness: all required elements and developed ideas. Clarity: understandable writing, with who/when/where/what for interviews. Progress: broad and deep exploration, explicit connections, and evidence of how ideas evolve. Go beyond documentation to interpretation and next steps; use pasted photos sparingly.

Schedule now
